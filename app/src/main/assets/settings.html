Turning it on asks for the microphone and starts Nana if she is off. Best with the offline wake model from Settings > Voice > Offline ears (no internet, no beeps). Without it she uses Google speech, which needs internet and the screen on. When she hears her name she pops up like the power button does, says Yes? and listens. "Last heard" shows what the phone understood, handy if it keeps missing.

She never types passwords, cards or OTPs and never acts unless you ask.

Watching

YouTube and Shorts: she reads the captions. TikTok, Instagram and others: only the public caption, so she tells you when she did not see the video itself. You can also share a link to Nana from any app. Watch along sends the visible text of video apps to your AI provider, only while it is on.

Ways to call her

Hold the power button or swipe from a corner (after choosing Nana as default assistant)
Say "Hey Nana" (turn it on above)
Quick Settings tile: edit tiles and add Call Nana
Notification buttons: Call / Hide / Stop
Select text in any app, then Ask Nana

Theme

Mono is white on dark and black on light. Black is pure AMOLED black. Her floating chat and radial menu use the accent but stay dark so they read on any app.
